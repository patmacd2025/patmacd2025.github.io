Neptune

Neptune is the furthest planet from the sun and is the fourth largest planet in the solar system.

It is 4.5 billion kilometers away from the sun and has a dimameter of 49,528 kilometers.

Neptune takes 16 hours to spin on it's axis and it takes 165 Earth years (60,191 Earth days)
to orbit the Sun.

Neptune is the only planet in our solar system not visible to the naked eye from Earth.

The sunlight we see here on Earth is roughly 900 times as bright as sunlight on Neptune.

Neptune has at least five main rings and four prominent ring arcs that we know of so far.

The gravitatiional pull of Neptune is 11m/s² which is 1.12 times that of Earth.
