Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the sun and is the second smallest planet in the solar system.

It is 228 milion kilometers away from the sun and has a diameter of 6,779 kilometers.

Mars rotates around it's axis every 24.6 hours and it orbits the sun every 687 Earth days or every
669.6 Martian days.

Mars has two moons that orbiy it, Phobos and Deimos.

Mars has no rings, but in 50 million years when Phobos crashes into it, the debris could form rings.

The temperatures on Mars have highs of 20°C and lows of -153°C.

The gravitational pull of Mars is 3.7m/s² which is 0.38 times that of Earth.
