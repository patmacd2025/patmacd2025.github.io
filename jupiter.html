Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and it it the largest planet in the solar system.

Jupiter is 778 million kilometers away from the Sun and has a diameter of 139,820 kilometers.

Jupiter's iconic Great Red Spot is a giant storm bigger than Earth that has raged for hundreds of years.

A day on Jupiter is only 9.9 hours, which is the shortest in the solar system.

It takes Jupiter around 12 Earth years (4,333 Earth days) to orbit the sun.

Jupiter has 4 large moons and over 90 recognised other smaller moons.

Jupiter's moon, Ganymede, is the largest moon in the solar system, and there could be a liquid ocean under the frozen surface of one of it's other moons, Europa, which could sustain life.

The gravitational pull of Jupiter is 23.1m/s² which is the higgest out of all the planets in the solar system, and it is 2.36 times that of Earth.
