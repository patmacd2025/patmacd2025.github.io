Earth

Earth is the only planet in the solar system that is known to support life. This is due to the fact that it is the perfect postition form the sun, not too close and not too far. Because of this it is sometimes refered to as a goldilocks planet.

Earth is the third planet from the sun and is the fourth smallest planet in the solar system.

It is 150 million kilometers away from the sun an it has a diameter of 12,756 kilometers.

Earth rotates on it's axis (1 day) every 23.9 hours and orbits the sun (1year) every 365.25 days.

Earth axis of rotation is tilted 23.4 degrees which causes different seasons, depending on which hemisphere is facing the sun.

Earth's atmosphere is 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen and 1% other ingredients. It's the perfect balance for us to breathe and live.

The gravitational pull of Earth is 9.8m/s².
