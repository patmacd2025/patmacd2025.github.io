Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the sun and it is the second largest planet in the solar system.

It has has a diameter of 120,500 kilometers and it is 1.4 bilion kilometers away from the Sun.

It takes Saturn 10.7 hours to spin on it's axis and 29.4 Earth years (10,756 Earth days) to orbit the sun.

Saturn's axis is tilted by 26.73 degrees which is similar to Earth's 23.4 degrees, so Saturn experiences seasons like Earth.

Saturn is known for it's large rings but it is not the only planet with rings.

Saturn's rings are pieces of comets, asteroids, and shattered moons that broke up before they reached the surface.

Saturn has 146 confirmed moons in it's orbit.

The gravitational pull Of Saturn is 9m/s² which is 0.92 that of Earth.
