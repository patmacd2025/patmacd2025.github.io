The Solar System

The Sun

The Sun is the largest object in our solar system with a radius of around 1,393,000 kilometers.

Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the sun and is the smallest planet in our solar system.

Venus

Venus is the hottest planet in the solar system. This is because of it's thick atmosphere that traps most of the heat.

Earth

Earth is the only planet in the solar system that is known to support life.

Mars

Mars has no rings, but in 50 million years when Phobos crashes into it, the debris could form rings.

Jupiter

Jupiter's iconic Great Red Spot is a giant storm bigger than Earth.

Saturn

Saturn has 146 confirmed moons in it's orbit.

Uranus

Uranus spins on it's axis with a tilt of 97.77 degrees, meaning that it orbits the sun sideways.

Neptune

The sunlight we see here on Earth is roughly 900 times as bright as sunlight on Neptune.
